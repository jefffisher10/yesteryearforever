Coastal Local Seafood

The coast, brought to Columbus.

Ohio may be a long way from the ocean, but you'd never know it at our counter.

COLO started with a simple idea: folks in Columbus deserve seafood as fresh as what you'd find on the docks. We source carefully from people we trust, favor sustainable and responsible practices, and keep the case full of the good stuff.

Pick something up for dinner, or pull up a stool and slurp a few on the half shell. Not sure what to cook? Just ask. That's half the fun.

Two ways to COLO

Take it home, or stay a while.

Shop the market case for tonight's dinner, or grab a seat at the bar and let us do the shucking.

The Market

Fresh fish, sea scallops, mussels, clams, shrimp and more, ready to take home. We're happy to help you pick and tell you how to cook it.

The Oyster Bar

A rotating lineup of East and West Coast oysters, shucked to order. Pull up a stool and ask what's tasting good today.

From the kitchen

Lobster rolls. Po' boys.

Hungry now? The kitchen turns out coastal classics made with the same seafood we sell in the case.

Meet the owner

Hi, I'm Ian.

I started COLO because I love great seafood, and I wanted Columbus to have a place where you could get it as fresh as you would on the coast, from people who actually know where it came from.

Most days you'll find me behind the counter, unloading the latest delivery or talking someone into trying a new oyster. Come say hi. I'll tell you what's good today.

— Ian, Owner
