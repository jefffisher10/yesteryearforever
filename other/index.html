Our Story

The coast, brought to Columbus.

Ohio may be a long way from the ocean, but you'd never know it at our counter. COLO (short for Coastal Local Seafood) started with a simple idea: folks in Columbus deserve seafood as fresh as what you'd find on the docks.

We source carefully from people we trust, favor sustainable and responsible practices, and keep the case full of the good stuff: fresh fish, shellfish, and a rotating lineup of oysters we're always happy to talk about. Stop in to pick something up for dinner, or pull up a seat and slurp a few on the half shell.

Not sure what to cook? Just ask. That's half the fun.
